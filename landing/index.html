Social-impact projects built and run for community initiatives

Bazno ♻️

Bazno turns recycling into social participation. People hand in their waste, earn participation coins for what they bring back into the cycle, and then choose what those coins fund — a social cause, a discount, or products in the shop. We built the whole platform, from the Persian right-to-left web app to the coin ledger behind it.

Full platform Coin ledger Persian · RTL Visit the platform →

Tahdig 🍲

Tahdig rescues food that would otherwise be thrown away. Restaurants and suppliers list what they have left at the end of the day, donors cover the cost, and couriers carry it to people who need it — so surplus food reaches a table instead of a bin.

Marketplace Courier logistics Persian Visit the site →

Soroush Mehr 🤝

A donation platform for the Soroush Mehr charity. Every fundraising campaign gets its own page with a clear goal and live progress, so supporters can see exactly what they are giving to and donate in a few taps.

Online donations Campaign pages Persian Visit the site →

Toloo Global 🌍

Toloo Global lets people living abroad support the Toloo Bi-neshanha Society back home. It solves the part that usually blocks cross-border giving: accepting international payments and getting them to a local charity that cannot easily receive them itself.
